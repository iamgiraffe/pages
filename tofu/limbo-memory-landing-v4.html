Why jobs get lost

The dangerous moment is not the call. It is what happens right after.

Field service work is chaotic by nature. You are in the truck, on a ladder, under a sink, on the shop floor, or in between jobs. That is exactly when details disappear.

“I’ll remember” is not a system

Half-finished texts, scraps of paper, and mental notes do not survive a busy day. The lead may feel safe in the moment, but it is still one interruption away from being lost.

If it never reaches the calendar, it is still at risk

A lead is not protected just because you heard it. It becomes real only when the details are captured, organized, and turned into an actual scheduled visit.

Missed callbacks feel small until the money is gone

The lost job is usually invisible at first. Then the competitor gets the work, the customer moves on, and the gap between intention and follow-up becomes a direct financial loss.
