The Solution

Four pillars of agentic payment infrastructure

1

Secure Transaction Routing

Intelligently route AI/IoT-initiated payments across networks. Real-time, fail-safe delivery. Multi-step orchestration: verify, check FX, execute.

2

Identity & Authorization

Validate agent identity with cryptographic mandates. Enforce real-time checks. Non-repudiable proof of intent for every autonomous transaction.

3

Integrated Fraud Detection

AI/ML anomaly detection across all CMA networks globally. Cross-network pattern recognition. Real-time flagging of suspicious behavior.

4

Future-Facing Interoperability

ISO 20022 standards. Connect SWIFT, cards, blockchain, stablecoins. Bridge PAPSS, BUNA, any regional network. Handle any conversion.
